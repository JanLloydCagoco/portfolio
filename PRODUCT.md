## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Recruiters and engineering leads evaluating a full-stack developer: they skim credentials, open projects, and evaluate technical craft.
- Government and institutional stakeholders evaluating public sector digital solutions and AI implementations.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jan Lloyd Cagoco, an Associate Developer at LGU Baggao, Cum Laude BS Computer Science graduate (CSU Carig), and Civil Service Professional. The portfolio showcases real-world projects (Baggao Official Website, CSM, Sari, Nicerice), verified credentials (Google AI Essentials), experience, and stack, providing direct contact via `/inquiry` and email.

Success: a visitor explores real work, understands technical craft, and reaches out.

## Positioning

A developer and designer's portfolio is itself a primary proof of craft: high performance, crisp typography, clean architecture, zero lag, and instant page transitions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, Turbopack. Fully static prerendering.
- Core Routes: `/` (Overview), `/projects`, `/experiences`, `/stack`, `/certifications`, `/inquiry` (API route `/api/contact` with Resend).
- Light, Dark, and live System theme modes (`Paper #FAF8F5` ground with warm `#141413` ink; Soft Graphite `#111214` ground with chalk `#EDEDEB` ink).
- Binding content rules: zero over-explanatory copy, zero eyebrows, zero decorative rules above buttons, pure CSS hardware-accelerated transforms (no bloated 3D or animation libraries).

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Every screen gets a visitor to a project faster.
2. Show, don't caption. If a line explains what the visitor can already see, cut it.
3. Fast by default. Sub-100ms transitions, zero bundle bloat, lean static pages.
4. Craft is the proof: type, motion, and depth hold up under close inspection.
